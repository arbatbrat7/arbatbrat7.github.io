Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

Nafilme Studio ("we") is an independent video studio. The Nafilme Studio application is a desktop tool used only by the studio itself to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channels.

2. What data the app accesses

When the studio owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the studio's finished videos to the selected channel;
  • youtube.readonly — to read the channel name and public statistics of the studio's own channels and videos (views, likes, subscriber count).

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, Drive, or any data of other people.

3. How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the studio's own computer and are used only to upload videos and read statistics of the studio's channels. Statistics are used to plan the next videos. We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models.

4. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings; the app also deletes the stored token when a channel is disconnected.

5. Viewers of our videos

We do not collect personal data of viewers. Videos for children are marked "made for kids" on YouTube.
